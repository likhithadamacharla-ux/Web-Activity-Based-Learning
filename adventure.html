Equipment Checklist

Things to Carry for Trekking

  • Backpack
  • Trekking Shoes
  • Water Bottle
  • Warm Clothes
  • Flashlight
  • First Aid Kit
  • Healthy Snacks
  • Cap and Sunglasses

Safety Guidelines

  • Always follow your trekking guide.
  • Carry enough drinking water.
  • Wear comfortable trekking shoes.
  • Do not travel alone on unknown routes.
  • Carry a first aid kit.
  • Do not throw plastic in forests.
  • Check weather conditions before starting.
